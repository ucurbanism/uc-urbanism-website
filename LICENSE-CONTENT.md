If you reuse the code, use your own Adobe Fonts kit or another font.

**If you fork this for another organization:** replace everything in the table above, and the Google Analytics ID,
calendar and social links in `src/content/`.

**If you are a future club officer:** you don't need any of this. The content is the club's, and you edit it as the
club. See "Taking over the site" in the [README](README.md).

To ask about reusing something, or to have a photo of yourself removed, open an issue on this repository or
message the club through one of the channels on the [Connect page](https://uc-urbanism.netlify.app/connect/).
