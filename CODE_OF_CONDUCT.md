# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful, assume good faith, accept feedback gracefully, and no harassment of any kind. It applies
to issues, pull requests, commit messages, and anywhere else people work on this site.

University of Cincinnati students are also bound by the university's Student Code of Conduct.

## Reporting

Tell a club officer in person, or message the club through the
[Connect page](https://uc-urbanism.netlify.app/connect/). Don't put details in a public issue. Reports are kept
confidential, and the board decides what happens next, following the Covenant's enforcement guidelines. A
maintainer who is the subject of a report takes no part in handling it.
